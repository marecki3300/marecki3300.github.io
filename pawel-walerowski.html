Formally trained in Poland and a graduate of the Conservatory of Poznań with a Master's Degree in Cello Performance, Paweł has built a career that spans continents, genres, and stages.

His early career in Europe saw him performing in ensembles across Poland, recording for radio and television, and receiving honors at the prestigious J.S. Bach Competition and the International Cello Competition in Austria. He served as Principal Cellist of the Youth String Orchestra in Slovenia and toured Europe with the Andrew Lloyd Webber Orchestra.

Coming to the United States to further his studies at The University of Arizona, Paweł quickly established himself across the country — performing as a cellist for Opera in the Ozarks in Arkansas, touring with an eclectic band through festivals and venues, and gracing San Francisco's renowned jazz club Yoshi's.

In the Bay Area, Paweł has recorded for singer-songwriters, performed with the Diablo Symphony, and contributed to the San Francisco Symphony's Adventures in Music Program — one of the longest-running orchestral education programs in the country.

Today, Paweł is a faculty member at Reeder Music Academy in Danville, California, where he teaches cello and piano to beginners and advanced students alike, sharing his love for classical, pop, and jazz styles.

"Music is the universal language — and my deepest joy is helping students find their own voice within it."

Paweł Walerowski

Teaching Specialisms

Cello & Piano

Cello
Primary Instrument · All Levels
Paweł's principal instrument, studied to Master's level and performed professionally across Europe and the United States. Lessons cover technique, posture, bow mechanics, tone production, and repertoire spanning baroque to contemporary. Both beginners and advanced students are warmly welcome.

Piano
Secondary Instrument · All Levels
An accomplished pianist, Paweł teaches piano alongside cello with equal care and depth. Lessons are tailored to the student's goals — classical foundation, music theory, sight-reading, or repertoire in a style of their choosing, from Chopin to jazz harmony.
